Synced mainline through b34646. This build adds group-scoped member hiding, alerts when selected group members speak, and individual durations for videos sent together. It also fixes member-settings navigation and state refresh, video saving to Photos, and retains the chat-list search improvements.

Hidden or filtered messages should stay excluded from alerts.
